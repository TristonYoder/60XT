# End of Challenge Reflection - Directors Cut
## Prelude: *Back* to Work
About a year before this challenge, I suffered from some pretty major back pain, and in the spring of 2025, I could not even walk without being in excruciating pain. Around that same time, my wife and I found out we were pregnant with our second child. After X-Rays and consults, I found a path toward recovery, through a combination of months of physical therapy, chiropractic care, and stretches. However at 265 lbs, it was also clear that I need to make other changes if I wanted to manage pain, and be in a place to be the husband and dad I wanted to be.

Throughout the rest of 2025 through pretty much exclusively through diet I worked my way down to 200lbs. At the end of the year I met with one of the Life Time trainers Jordan, after a goal-setting session, where we discussed my desire to build strength, and resilience against back issues, but also nervousness around making my back worse in the process, he recommended that I take the ALPHA class. I started taking 1 or 2 a week, and honestly found the biggest impact it had on me was building trust that the coaches in the class would not let me lift in a way that would make my back worse.

Jump forward a couple of months, our second child was born, nearing the end of my paternity leave. At this point the combination of stressors, diet, and sleeplessness that come with a newborn had taken their toll, and I was putting on weight. Honestly, I made my peace with it, physical regression was just part of “this season of life”.
Leading up to 60XT the trainers in the ALPHA classes talked a bit about 60XT. One evening I was talking with my wife about 60XT, and I mentioned how it would be a good challenge if it didn’t start right as my leave was ending and I was going back to work. If I couldn’t keep up with training and diet on leave, it would be crazy to do a transformation challenge when I was returning to work.

She challenged me on that, and pointed out that for someone who thought it was a bad idea, I sure did seem to be thinking about it a whole lot, and if we could figure out how to raise two kids, we could probably figure out a training schedule and diet changes. So in the first week of April, I returned to work and started the 60XT challenge.
## Goals
During the challenge I had three goals. The first is simple on paper, body recomposition in my case, dropping fat while retaining as much muscle as possible. My second goal was to find a recovery rhythm. Serving as the night caretaker so my wife could sleep after a long day, I knew finding intentional recovery rhythms would be incredibly important. Since my sleep was actively being broken into small chunks with nighttime feedings, I needed to support recovery as best as I could in other ways. Finally, I needed to find a training rhythm that allowed me to make progress, while supporting my family well in this transition.

## The Role of Life Time in my Transformation
Life Time served as a critical space for me in this challenge. For my 60XT consult I met up with Jordan again, and we went over diet, and we were able to troubleshoot an issue in how I was measuring my food that led me to consistently under calculating my caloric intake. That meeting alone was the reason my progress didn’t stay stalled, and allowed me to work toward recomposition.

The fitness floor itself is comprehensive, because of this I was able to find all of the stretching and recovery equipment that were part of my lower-back recovery process. Being able to continue managing my recovery as a part of my workout rhythm meant I could more effectively maximize my time by doing everything in one place.
In my goal to maximize recovery I made use of the sauna, cold plunge, and steam room. Prior to 60XT I had never done a cold plunge, but in the spirit of the challenge, I tried it, and it’s a new and continuous tool in my recovery rhythm. All three of those tools made a huge impact on recovery.

The ALPHA course was probably the single most physically impactful thing for me in this challenge, it provided a consistent, planned, and regimented training program. Beyond that the community in the classes are incredible, and I found myself looking forward to class every morning. I was able to do the ALPHA programming 6 Days a week, and do the morning class so I could get my workout in before the rest of my family got up for the day. Every coach I interacted with throughout the challenge brought a unique flare and insight to the challenge. Tracy, Issy, and Sam pushed me towards endurance and consistent form throughout every workout, and I greatly value their guidance, and form correction as I learn new movements.

Part way through the challenge my three year old wanted to start going to the gym too on the weekends. Once that shift happened, even the Saturday classes a bit later in the day made for a light load on my wife since our son could go with me. One of these Saturdays was actually one of the most special for my son and I. We did a “Dada - Judah Date”, where we went to our respective classes, and then ate lunch together in the Life Cafe after our classes, before going and spending time at the pool. That connection time was so sweet and made Life Time a special place for both of us. The integration of my family into my fitness journey was critical to making this transformation possible.

Throughout the challenge the 60XT podcast episodes were also valuable and encouraging. The insights specifically on data gathering and tracking were really interesting, and I started to be way more intentional about how I gathered my data, which helped paint a more clear picture of my progress in the later phase of the challenge. Tackling the issue of identity also spoke to me, and I realized part of what was happening with this challenge, was my re-identifying of myself as “someone who cares for their body”. The quick check-ins also just kept me feeling hyped during the challenge because I had someone to share the wins with without needing to think about any social norms. 
## What Changed
### Physical Results
I started the challenge at 205.6 lbs and ended at 192.8 lbs. (-12.8 lbs)
I started the challenge at 18.2% of Body Fat and ended at 13.5% of Body Fat (-4.7%)
I started the challenge at ~50ms HRV and ended at ~100ms HRV
In march my average Resting Heart Rate was 65 bpm in May it was 49 bpm
I started the challenge a couple of pants sizes larger, and dropped to Medium T-Shirt Sizes

All of the physical changes were outward indicators of what was going on behind the scenes though.
### Mental Shift
I completed Monday-Saturday Alpha programming with 100% consistency, even when out of town for work. My diet was on point. On Sundays I did a 60XT Cardio workout. My confidence in my ability to workout without injury, and confidence that I can and will show up for myself are higher than ever. The literal physical changes alongside the mental shift in my identity has made me a better husband, dad, manager, everything. I’m in the best shape I’ve ever been in, and while I still have more that I want to accomplish, but I’m proud of what I’ve been able to achieve over these last 60 days.

Really committing to the ALPHA courses for 60XT and finding a path to make it sustainable for my family also gives me the confidence that I’ve found something that will work long term.
