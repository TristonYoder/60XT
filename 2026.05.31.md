# 60XT Essay
## Requirements
The essay should explain your goals during the 60XT, how Life Time helped you achieve your results—including Life Time facilities, services, programming and/or 60XT components —how your lifestyle changed, and how you plan to maintain these changes. The essay should focus primarily on your time during the 60XT. **The essay must be a minimum of 2,500 characters and is limited to 5,000 characters** (“60XT Essay”). If the 60XT Essay exceeds 5,000 characters, only the first 5,000 characters will be read and used by Life Time. Life Time reserves the right to redact portions of any essay before publishing. Please do not use other brand or trade names in your essay because they will not be printed. [KW1]
